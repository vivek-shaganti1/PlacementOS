# Review and tooling, 2026-11-12

Sections A to F of the production checklist, reviewed by four independent read-only reviewers. Each finding was then
fixed here with a test that fails on the old code, or left to the owner with the reason. Items already in
`SECURITY_RISK_REGISTER.md` are not repeated. Everything ran locally with synthetic data. The only live-site checks were
read-only page loads (Lighthouse) and public endpoint checks.
